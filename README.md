# 🕎 Avodah — Beit HaMikdash

A 3D educational game for kids. You play a young Kohen in the Beit HaMikdash and do a full day of the Avodah, in the order of Mishnah Tamid, from before sunrise to the golden lamps of Simchat Beit HaShoevah at night.

**Play:** https://y2jcpa.github.io/beit-hamikdash/

## What's in the game

### The world
The Beit HaMikdash is laid out to the proportions of Mishnah Middot (1 amah ≈ ½ m):

- **Ezrat Nashim** is 135 by 135 amot, as wide as the Azara (Middot 2:5). It has its balcony, the four 40-amah corner chambers, the Leviim's instrument rooms under Ezrat Yisrael (Middot 2:6), and the 50-amah golden lamp towers of Simchat Beit HaShoevah, which light up at night.
- **The 15 semicircular steps** lead up to **Nikanor Gate** and its bronze doors.
- **The Azara** has the Duchan with the Leviim and their instruments, and gilded gates with Hebrew signs. Its six lishkot are where Middot 5:3-4 puts them: salt, parvah and rinsing in the north; wood, the Golah and **Lishkat HaGazit** in the south. There is also the lamb chamber.
- **Lishkat HaGazit** is a basilica (Yoma 25a). The 71 sages of the Sanhedrin sit inside in half a circle, with their two scribes in front (Sanhedrin 4:3).
- **The Mizbeach** is 32 amot of whitewashed whole stones. It has the Kevesh (ramp), the red line (chut ha-sikra), the four kranot, the ash heap, three fires, the silver shitin bowls and the salt pile.
- **The Kiyor** has Ben Katin's 12 spouts.
- **Beit HaMitbachayim** has 24 rings, 8 pillars with hooks and 8 marble tables.
- **The Ulam** is 100 amot tall with a gold-plated front, the **golden vine** with its grape clusters, *kalah orev* spikes, golden chains, and Queen Helena's golden lamp.
- **The Heichal** can be walked into. It has gold relief walls, a cedar ceiling, light shafts from the windows, the **Menorah**, the **Shulchan** with the 12 loaves, the **Mizbeach HaZahav**, and the woven **Paroches**. The Kodesh HaKodashim is behind it and can't be entered.
- **Har HaBayit** is shown with its colonnades, the Soreg and the Chel. **Yerushalayim** is around it, and Har HaZeitim covered in olive groves is to the east.
- **The sky** follows a real sun path through the day: stars and moonlight before dawn, the "Barkai!" sunrise, then midday and night.

### The daily Avodah
The quests follow Mishnah Tamid, simplified for kids:

1. 💧 Washing hands and feet at the **Kiyor**
   - 🎲 Then the **Payis**, the morning lottery (Yoma 2). The Kohanim stand in a circle and hold up one finger each (we count fingers, never people). The Memuneh calls out a number and counts around the circle. If it lands on you, you win that day's honor (Terumat HaDeshen, the Tamid, the Menorah, the Ketoret or the Nesachim), worth 2 bonus stars.
2. 🌅 **Terumat HaDeshen**: take the silver machta, scoop the ashes, and place them east of the ramp
3. 🪵 **Siddur Ma'arachah**: bring wood up to the fire. Then the watchman calls ***"Barkai!"*** and the sun rises.
4. 🕎 **Hatavat HaNerot**, in the Mishnah's order: before the Tamid, prepare five lamps. The two eastern lamps are still burning, and you leave them for now (Tamid 3:9; Yoma 33a).
5. 🐑 **Korban Tamid**: take a lamb from the lamb chamber, check it for blemishes, and give it water from the golden cup. Then do shechita in the north, kabbalah, holacha, zerika (two placements that are four: NE then SW), shirayim on the southern yesod, salting, and haktarah.
6. 💨 **Ketoret** on the golden altar (the goats of Yericho sneeze 🐐). First you go back in for the last two lamps: prepare the eastern one and leave the **Ner Ma'aravi** burning (Tamid 6:1).
7. 🙌 **Birkat Kohanim** from the steps of the Ulam. The crowd bows and answers *Baruch Shem*.
8. 🎵 **Nesachim and the Shir Shel Yom**: pour the wine, then play the real weekday's song with the Leviim in a rhythm game. At each pause the trumpets sound and everyone bows.
9. 🌙 **On Rosh Chodesh, the Musaf**: count out the right animals (2 bulls, a ram, 7 lambs and a goat), blow the chatzotzrot over the korbanot (Bamidbar 10:10), and bring the parts to the fire.

### The Hebrew calendar 📅
The game works out today's Hebrew date on the device, with no internet needed.
- On Rosh Chodesh the Musaf is added to the day automatically, and the title screen says so.
- The **📅 Calendar** tab in the Sefer shows today's date and what's coming up: Rosh Chodesh, Shabbat and the holidays. The Rosh Chodesh Musaf can be practised there any time.
- Nobody plays on Shabbat, Yom Tov or Yom Kippur, so holidays will be playable any day, to practise before them.

### Visitors with their own korbanot
Each day 4 of these 9 visitors come, and ones you haven't met yet come first.
- Reuven brings an **Olah**.
- Miriam brings a **turtledove**. *"Whether one brings a lot or a little…"*
- Yehuda brings a **Chatat**: four horns in order SE, NE, NW, SW, and the Kohanim eat the meat.
- A family brings a **Shelamim**. It can be slaughtered anywhere in the Azara, and you wave the parts in the **tenufah** game.
- Chana brings a **Mincha** for the **kemitzah** timing game.
- A farmer from the Galil brings **Bikkurim**, with an ox with golden horns.
- Binyamin came home safely from a stormy sea voyage and brings a **Todah**, with its 40 breads. The Kohen gets one of each kind.
- Naftali brings an **Asham**: he kept his neighbor's tools and said they weren't his. He gave them back with an extra fifth.
- Elisheva brings a **Bechor**, her sheep's firstborn lamb. It has no semicha and one placement, on a corner that has a yesod (the south-east corner has none).

When everyone has been helped, you unlock the **eight garments of the Kohen Gadol**. The bells on the me'il really jingle when you walk.

### 🗝️ Secret missions in the chambers
Explore and you'll find people in the chambers who need help. A mission can be done once the day's Avodah is done, or right between two avodot. The next avodah waits for you.
- **Lishkat HaEtzim (wood):** a Kohen with a mum checks the wood for worms (Middot 2:5). Take out the wormy logs, the olive wood and the grapevine (Tamid 2:3), then carry the good logs to the wood pile.
- **Lishkat HaNezirim:** Gidon finishes his nezirut. His hair goes into the fire under the pot of his Shelamim, then you do the tenufah with the cooked foreleg (Nazir 6:8-9).
- **Lishkat HaMetzora'im:** Yoav, who was healed, immerses and then waits at Nikanor Gate. You put the dam of his Asham on his right ear, thumb and big toe, in order (Negaim 14:8-9).
- **Beit Shemanya (oil and wine):** measure half a log of oil for each lamp of the Menorah (Menachot 89a), and the wine for the nesachim (Bamidbar 28:14).
- **The Leviim's instrument rooms:** Uziel the Levi plays a tune on the kinor, nevel, chalil and cymbals, and you play it back (Middot 2:6; Arachin 2:3-6).
- **Lishkat HaGazit, the Sanhedrin:** a sage tells what happens when the Sanhedrin rules by mistake and Israel follows it. You bring the **Par He'elem Davar** (Vayikra 4:13-21):
  - three sages do semicha (Sanhedrin 1:3; Rabbi Yehuda says five)
  - shechita in the north
  - the dam is sprinkled seven times toward the Paroches and put on the horns of the golden Mizbeach. As Kohen Gadol you do this yourself; otherwise you watch the Kohen Gadol.
  - the rest of the dam goes on the western yesod
  - the emurim go on the fire, and the bull is burned outside Yerushalayim (Zevachim 5:2)

### Coming back every day
- **🔥 Esh Tamid.** "The fire on the Mizbeach shall never go out" (Vayikra 6:6). Every day you play, the fire on the Mizbeach grows. Shabbat and Yom Tov never break the streak, and a missed day only makes the fire smaller; it never goes out.
- **💎 The Choshen.** Every visitor comes from one of the 12 tribes. Help visitors to earn their tribes' stones; the Leviim give you Levi's stone after the Shir Shel Yom. Up to 2 new stones come each day, so it takes about a week.
- **👑 Kohen Gadol for a week.** With all 12 stones you serve as Kohen Gadol for 7 days:
  - you wear the 8 garments
  - you bring the **Minchat Chavitin** every morning
  - you offer the Tamid with the full ceremony of Tamid 7:3 (the Segan at your right, Ben Arza's cymbal)
  - for Birkat Kohanim you raise your hands only up to the Tzitz

  Then you go back to being a Kohen and collect the stones again.
- **📜 The Memunim (Shekalim 5:1).** After each full day of Avodah, Elazar appoints you as a helper to another officer of the Mikdash:
  - **Gevini the Crier:** the morning call
  - **Ben Gever:** closing the gates at night
  - **Beit Garmu:** baking the Lechem HaPanim on Erev Shabbat

  Tap your player card to see everything in **💎 My Path**.

### Learning built in
- **Mistakes teach.** If you try to slaughter an Olah in the south, Elazar the Memuneh explains why it must be in the north, with the source (Zevachim 5:1).
- **📖 Sefer HaMikdash (journal)** has three sections:
  - 23 places to discover
  - 9 miracles to find (the smoke pillar that the wind never scattered, the warm Lechem HaPanim…)
  - every lesson you've learned, each with its source
- There are 25 achievements, stars, and titles from *Young Kohen* up to *Kohen Gadol*.

## Controls
| | Keyboard / mouse | Phone / tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | WASD / arrows | put your thumb anywhere on the left half and slide (the joystick appears under it) |
| Run | Shift | slide the joystick all the way out |
| Act / hold | E (hold for "hold" actions) | the big gold button (it shows the icon of what you can do), or tap the prompt |
| Look | drag the mouse, scroll to zoom | drag on the right half, pinch to zoom |
| Guide me | G / 🧭 Guide me | 🧭: your Kohen walks to the next task by himself (move the joystick to stop) |
| Jump | Space | ⤒ |
| Sefer | J | 📖 |

### Made for phones
- **Text boxes get out of the way.**
  - "Did you know?" cards appear as a small pill: tap to read, swipe or ✕ to close. They're always saved in the Sefer.
  - The task card folds itself into one line after a few seconds (tap to open it again).
  - 👁 hides all the text at once.
- **Guide me (🧭).** It plans a walking route through the Mikdash, up the 15 steps, the Kevesh and the Ulam steps, and walks you there. Every quest target is covered by an automated route test.
- **Stays smooth.** Resolution adjusts itself to keep the frame rate up, and glow and shadows switch off on slow phones. Holding the phone upright gives a wider camera view.
- **Can be installed, and works offline.** Add it to the home screen to play full-screen. Once it has been opened online, it also opens with no internet (in the car, on a trip). In ⚙️ Settings you can choose the card size, read-aloud, and full screen on Android.
- **עברית.** The whole game can be played in Hebrew, right to left, with Hebrew read-aloud and sources written the traditional way (זבחים ה:א). All the Hebrew has full nikud for children learning to read, and it can be turned off in ⚙️ Settings (ניקוד). Use the 🌐 button on the title screen or ⚙️ Settings. Phones set to Hebrew start in Hebrew.

## Tech
- Vanilla ES modules with **Three.js r170 vendored** in `vendor/three/`. No build step and no CDN; it runs straight from GitHub Pages.
- All textures are **painted procedurally** when the game loads: Herodian ashlars with drafted margins, polished flagstones, gold relief, the woven paroches. All sounds are **synthesized live** with Web Audio: Karplus-Strong kinor and nevel, silver trumpets, cymbals, fire crackle, birds.
- Rendering:
  - physical sky with image-based lighting (gold looks like gold)
  - ACES tone mapping, soft shadows and bloom
  - shader fire and a smoke pillar that rises straight up
  - instanced crowds, city and olive groves
  - characters merged into a few draw calls each
- There are three quality levels (Fast / Nice / Beautiful). The game picks one automatically and you can change it in ⚙️ Settings.
- Up to 10 profiles are saved in `localStorage`.

# Notes & Known Limitations — V3

V3 is a full rebuild of the V2 voxel prototype. All of the V2 items are fixed by the rebuild: movement direction, walking up the ramp, the korban flow, guidance, fire and particle effects, sound, the favicon, the camera, waypoints, Menachot, the Heichal interior, the Kohen Gadol, and more NPCs.

## Simplifications (on purpose, for kids)
- Shechita is shown as a soft glow and sparkles. There is no knife or animal harm on screen, and the dam is shown only as a small mark.
- The order of the morning Avodah follows Mishnah Tamid but is shortened. The Menorah is now in its real order (five lamps before the Tamid, the last two before the Ketoret), but the eivarim are still burned right after zerika instead of after the Ketoret and Birkat Kohanim.
- The Menorah stands east to west, as the Mishnah in Tamid holds (Bartenura), so the Ner Ma'aravi is the second lamp from the east. The Rambam holds it stood across the Heichal, with the middle lamp as the Ner Ma'aravi. For a Chabad Kohen the game follows the Rambam: straight branches, the Menorah across the Heichal, the middle lamp as the Ner Ma'aravi, and five lamps starting from it. The Rambam doesn't say which four join it, so the player chooses. The stone with three steps stays on the north side of the Menorah for everyone.
- Ashkenazi spelling covers the common words (Beis, Shabbos, korbanos, Ketores, the books in the sources). It only changes English; the Hebrew is the same for every tradition.
- The Sanhedrin's Par He'elem Davar follows Rabbi Meir (one bull, brought by the court) and three sages for semicha. The burning outside Yerushalayim is shown as a short cinematic.
- Hefshet and nituach (flaying and cutting), the chavitin, and the afternoon Tamid are not playable yet.
- The Ezrat Kohanim and Ezrat Yisrael are drawn at the same floor level.
- The Payis picks your "honor" for the day (worth bonus stars), but you still help with every avodah. In the real Mikdash there were several lotteries, and only the winner did each avodah (Yoma 2).
- The Rosh Chodesh Musaf is shortened: you count out the animals, blow the chatzotzrot, and bring the parts to the fire, but you don't do each animal's avodah.
- The game's Hebrew date changes at midnight, not at sunset.

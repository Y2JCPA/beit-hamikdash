Privacy

Avodah uses Google Analytics to understand how many people play, which devices they use, and how they found the game. This information helps improve the game.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and interactions with the site. We send gameplay events such as starting a game, completing a task or an Avodah day, and reaching active-play time milestones. We do not send player names, profile identifiers, or saved-game files to Google Analytics. Player profiles and saved progress stay in the browser on the player’s device.
